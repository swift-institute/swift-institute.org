Layer 1: single concepts that every higher layer relies on.

## Overview

An atom is one irreducible concept, such as addition, an angle or an ASCII code unit. Atoms depend only on other atoms and do not import Foundation.

Atoms are published under the [swift-atoms](https://github.com/swift-atoms) organization as `swift-{concept}`.
