Layer 2: data structures and mechanisms built from atoms.

## Overview

A molecule combines atoms into a larger building block, such as an array, an async channel or a modular algebra. Molecules depend on atoms and other molecules and do not import Foundation.

Molecules are published under the [swift-molecules](https://github.com/swift-molecules) organization as `swift-{concept}`.
